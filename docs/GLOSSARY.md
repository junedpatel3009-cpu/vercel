# Glossary

- **Client**: customer who posts work and hires a professional.
- **Professional**: service provider who lists services and applies for work.
- **Admin**: privileged role for platform operations and content management.
- **Client job**: marketplace job posted by a client.
- **Project request/application**: professional's bid/cover letter for a job.
- **Direct hire**: contract flow represented by `HireJob`, `HireContract`, and milestones.
- **Tracking ID**: numeric identifier used for project activity, transactions and reviews.
- **CMS**: managed website/legal page content.
- **Wallet/payout**: internal balance and withdrawal-request records; not evidence of an external payment rail.
- **SSR**: server-side rendering via TanStack Start.
